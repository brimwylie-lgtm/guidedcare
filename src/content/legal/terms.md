## About GuidedCare

GuidedCare provides independent information and navigation support to families arranging care for an older adult in Ontario. GuidedCare does not provide health care, personal care or home care services.

## Not clinical, legal or financial advice

GuidedCare is not a regulated health professional, social worker, lawyer, accountant, financial advisor or mediator. Nothing on this website or in any GuidedCare plan is a medical, legal, tax or financial opinion.

- Questions about health should go to the person's physician, nurse practitioner, pharmacist or Ontario Health atHome care coordinator.
- Questions about powers of attorney, capacity, contracts, estates or property should go to a lawyer.
- Questions about taxes, benefits or finances should go to a qualified accountant or advisor.

## No assessment of care needs

GuidedCare does not assess care needs, determine eligibility or recommend a level of care. Eligibility for publicly funded home care and long-term care is determined by Ontario Health atHome. Options are presented for the family's information; decisions rest with the individual or their substitute decision-maker.

## Independence

GuidedCare is paid only by the families it serves. It accepts no commissions, referral fees or other compensation from care providers, retirement homes, long-term care homes or vendors. Organizations named on this website or in any plan are listed for information only and are not endorsed.

## Accuracy

Program rules, costs, waitlists and contact details change. Information on this website was checked on the date shown and should be confirmed with the organization named before acting on it.

## Emergencies

GuidedCare is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

## Booking and payment

- The 15-minute fit call is free and carries no obligation.
- Paid services are invoiced after the fit call, once the family has chosen a path. Sessions are booked after the invoice is paid.
- Each paid service has its own written engagement agreement, which sets out its scope, time limits, cancellation and refund terms.
- Work beyond a package's time limit is billed only with the client's written approval first.

## Consent

For Navigation and the monthly retainer, GuidedCare requires signed consent from the parent, or their substitute decision-maker if the parent is not capable, before contacting anyone on the parent's behalf. If consent cannot be obtained, GuidedCare supports the family only and does not contact providers about the parent.
