heading: Book a free 15‑minute fit call
intro: A short, no-pressure call. We'll explain what GuidedCare does, hear the short version of what's going on, and tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.
beforeCall:
  - Your name, email and phone number
  - What's happening, in a sentence or two
  - How urgent it is (this week, this month, or planning ahead)
  - Which city your parent lives in
  - A few times that suit you for a 15-minute call
afterCall:
  - We'll reply to set a time for your call. Calls are by phone or video, Monday to Friday.
  - On the call, we'll suggest one path. That might be a free service, a Roadmap or Navigation. You decide, and there's no obligation.
  - If you choose a Roadmap or Navigation, we'll send you an invoice and book your session once it's paid. Nothing is paid online.
  - For a Roadmap, we'll also send a short intake form (about 15 minutes) to complete at least 48 hours before your session. It doesn't ask for your parent's name, health details or medications.
