GuidedCare is a small independent business in Halton Region, Ontario. This policy explains what personal information I collect, why, and how I protect it. Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) governs how GuidedCare handles personal information. GuidedCare is not a health information custodian under Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA) and does not provide health care.

## Privacy contact

Brian, GuidedCare. Email [bri.mwylie@gmail.com](mailto:bri.mwylie@gmail.com). Contact me with any question, to see the information I hold about you, to correct it, or to ask me to delete it.

## What I collect

**When you book a fit call:** your name, email, phone number, and your answers to three short questions (what's happening, how urgent it is, and which city).

**For a Roadmap:** your contact details and a short intake form about your parent's situation. The form is designed so your parent cannot be identified. It asks for an age range, municipality, living situation, daily tasks that are getting harder, current support and paperwork status. It does not ask for your parent's name, birth date, address, diagnoses or medications.

**For Navigation and the monthly retainer:** information needed to act on your family's behalf, collected only after your parent, or their substitute decision-maker, has signed a consent form. I will not contact any provider about your parent without that consent.

**On this website:** cookie-free analytics that count page visits without identifying you. This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The booking calendar is provided by Cal.com and loads only when you choose to open it.

## What I never collect

Health card numbers, Social Insurance Numbers, birth dates, diagnoses, medication lists or financial account details.

## How I use it

Only to deliver GuidedCare's services to you: to schedule and hold calls, prepare your Roadmap or plan, send invoices and keep basic business records. I do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

## Who sees it

Only me. Your information is stored with service providers I use to run the business (for example, scheduling, forms, email and accounting tools). Some of these providers may store information outside Canada.

## How I protect it

- Tools with encryption and two-factor login
- Completed intake forms are not sent as email attachments
- Session notes are kept to a minimum

## How long I keep it

Records are deleted 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask me to delete them sooner or the law requires me to keep them longer (for example, financial records for tax purposes).

## Email updates

If you sign up for updates about the Halton directory, I'll only email you about that, and every email will include a way to unsubscribe.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
