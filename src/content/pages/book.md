heading: Book a free 15‑minute fit call
intro: A short, no-pressure call. I'll explain what I do, hear the short version of what's going on, and tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.
beforeCall:
  - What's happening, in a sentence or two
  - How urgent it is (this week, this month, or planning ahead)
  - Which city your parent lives in
afterCall:
  - You'll get a confirmation email with the call details, and a reminder before the call.
  - On the call, I'll suggest one path. That might be a free service, a Roadmap or Navigation. You decide, and there's no obligation.
  - If you choose a Roadmap or Navigation, I'll send you an invoice and a link to book your session once it's paid. Nothing is paid online.
  - For a Roadmap, I'll also send a short intake form (about 15 minutes) to complete at least 48 hours before your session. It doesn't ask for your parent's name, health details or medications.
