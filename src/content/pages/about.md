## Independent, and paid only by families

GuidedCare is paid only by the families it serves. We accept no commissions, referral fees or other payment from care providers, retirement homes, long-term care homes or vendors. When we mention an organization, it's for your information, not an endorsement.

## Alongside free services, not instead of them

Halton has good free navigation services, including Ontario Health atHome, Links2Care and the Connected Care Halton Navigation Line. We see them as partners. Part of every Roadmap is making sure you're using every free service your family is entitled to. If those are all you need, we'll tell you on the fit call.

## What we do and don't do

We help families understand their options, prepare for assessments and conversations, and connect with the right services. We are not nurses, doctors, social workers, lawyers or financial advisors. We don't assess care needs, determine eligibility or recommend a level of care. Eligibility for publicly funded home care and long-term care is decided by Ontario Health atHome, and decisions rest with your parent or their substitute decision-maker.

When something needs a professional, such as a lawyer for a power of attorney or an accountant for tax questions, we'll say so and note it as an action in your plan.
